# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all contributors, regardless of background, experience, or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of others' time, ideas, and perspectives.
- Assume good intent, but address impact directly.
- Provide constructive feedback focused on code and ideas, not people.
- Help newer contributors succeed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hostile language.
- Deliberately submitting unverified work (including AI-generated code presented as your own).
- Attempting to circumvent project scope or integrity policies.
- Trolling or dismissing legitimate concerns.

## Enforcement

Violations of this code of conduct may result in:

- Warnings or removal of comments
- Temporary or permanent ban from the project

## Special note on contribution integrity

We take dishonest contributions seriously because they waste maintainer time and break trust. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for our AI disclosure and integrity policy.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience a violation, please report it privately to the project maintainers. We take all reports seriously and will handle them confidentially.

---

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.
