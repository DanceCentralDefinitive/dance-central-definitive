# Support

Need help with Dance Central Definitive? Here's where to get it.

## Community

**Get help and connect with other modders:**

- **[Dance Central Definitive Discord](https://discord.gg/Bj94CECgcK)** — Community focused on Dance Central Definitive. Share your projects, ask about mod tooling, and discuss workflow challenges.
- **[MiloHax Discord](https://discord.gg/milohax)** — Primary community for rhythm game modding, DTA scripting, and Harmonix format questions. Great for format-specific, technically in-depth and cross-game questions.

Most questions get answered quickly in either community.

## Resources

### Documentation & References

**Learning DTA and mod tooling:**

- **[Library of DTA Scripts](https://github.com/hmxmilohax/milo-script-library)** (hmxmilohax) — Browse real DTA examples across various Harmonix games to understand patterns and conventions.
- **[Dance Central 3 Decomp](https://github.com/rjkiv/dc3-decomp)** — Source code reference for understanding the inner structure and mechanics of Dance Central 3.
- **[Dance Central Definitive Docs](https://dancecentraldefinitive.github.io/dance-central-definitive/)** — Beginner-friendly documentation for basic Dance Central concepts and structure.
- **[Introduction to Data Array](https://mariteaux.somnolescent.net/modding/guitar-hero/tutorials/an-introduction-to-data-array/)** (Mariteaux) — Well-written general article on DTA format history and structure.

For more detailed information on agents and workflows, see [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Issues and bugs

If you've found a bug in the build system or tooling:

1. Check [existing issues](../../issues) — your bug might already be reported.
2. [Open a new bug report](../../issues/new?template=bug_report.md) with exact reproduction steps.
3. Include your OS, the commands you ran, and the error output.

## Feature requests

Have an idea for an improvement?

[Submit a feature request](../../issues/new?template=feature_request.md) describing the problem and your proposed solution.

## Documentation improvements

Spotted something unclear or outdated in the docs?

[Suggest a documentation improvement](../../issues/new?template=documentation.md).
